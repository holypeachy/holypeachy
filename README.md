# 🍑 holypeach

**`Software Engineer`**  
**`I build whatever I find interesting: backends, web apps, games, bots, tools, and little low-level experiments.`**

I’ve played around with computers all my life, so programming eventually became the natural next step. I’ve been experimenting with programming since high school, that's about 8 or 9 years now. Over the past 4 years, I’ve started taking on larger projects, both solo and with other developers. I'm also part of a small game dev team I formed with friends from university: [TeaPot TopHats](https://github.com/TeaPot-TopHats). We occasionally get together for game jams.

I like to know how things work. I care about architecture, simplicity, and docs. I hate black boxes, and I enjoy building things from scratch. Right now, my focus is Go and lower-level systems programming. One of my dreams is to contribute to the Linux kernel, which means C and Rust are definitely on the roadmap.

Fun fact, I didn't study Computer Science but rather Biochemistry and Molecular Biology. I'm a scientist at heart but my mind loves building things. I also enjoy my tools. My OS is Fedora KDE and live in the terminal, where I code using the Helix editor. I've tried to move to Neovim but it's so much work, maybe one day.
